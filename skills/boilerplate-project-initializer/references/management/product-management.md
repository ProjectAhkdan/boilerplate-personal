# Product Management Interview

## Question Bank
- **Vision**: Product Vision, Problem Statement, Target Users?
- **Goals**: Business Goals, Success Metrics?
- **Scope**: MVP definition, Priorities (P0 Critical, P1 Important, P2 Nice to Have, P3 Future)?
- **Execution**: User Stories, Acceptance Criteria, Dependencies, Risks?
- **Exclusions**: Explicit Out-of-Scope items for current phase?
